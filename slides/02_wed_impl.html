Biconditionals

We have a special operator for those implications whose converses are also True:

  \((p\rightarrow q) \wedge (q\rightarrow p)\equiv p\leftrightarrow q\)

Some phrases describing this relationship:

  • biconditional
  • if and only if (iff)
  • equivalent
