Predicates


\(p\): ___________ ate cereal for breakfast.


To apply the statement to many students, define a Predicate.


  • \(P(x)\): \(x\) ate cereal for breakfast.

  • \(x\) can be instantiated to a particular student, or an arbitrary one.

  • \(P(\underline{\hspace{2em}})\) is a proposition.

Boolean Masks

Select all cereals with at least 4 units of protein and no more than 6 units of sugars.

A claim about the data


Every cereal with at least 4 units of protein has at most 6 units of sugars.


True or false?


How would you establish it? __________________

Two ways to settle it

Check every row

(~p | q).all()

Returns True: settled.

Find one row that fails

cereals[p & ~q].head(1)

Returns a row: settled.


Written in logic:

\[\forall x\, \big(P(x) \rightarrow Q(x)\big) \qquad\qquad \exists x\, \big(P(x) \wedge \neg Q(x)\big)\]

The dataset is finite, so .all() checks every case. That is a proof. One counterexample refutes. Do not editorialize; just let them see that the pandas call and the quantifier are the same statement.

Now change one thing


Same claim, but about every cereal ever manufactured.


Does .all() still settle it? ______

Does one counterexample still settle it? ______


What could you do instead? __________________


Week 1, the card game: you could see all six cards. That is the only reason it could be settled.

Refutation survives; verification does not. When the domain is too big to enumerate you sample, and the verdict changes from settled to supported. That is claim B from lecture 1, and it is the rest of their degree.

Where it breaks

Add a cereal whose protein is missing.

cereals.loc[len(cereals)] = {'name': 'Mystery', 'protein': None, 'sugars': 3}
p = cereals['protein'] < 4
q = cereals['sugars'] > 6
((~p) & (~q)).sum(), (~(p | q)).sum()


De Morgan held in the truth table. Do the two counts agree? ______

Why? __________________

Missing data gives a third value. Two-valued laws are not guaranteed. Connects back to lecture 2: a proposition is True or False — NaN is neither, so it is not one.
